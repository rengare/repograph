repograph

Open a graph exported with rkg export — the repo.edges and nodes.tsv pair. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Rendering uses WebGPU. It works best in an up-to-date Chrome or Edge (113+), where WebGPU is on by default on Windows and macOS — no setup needed. Firefox's WebGPU is early and much slower today. Serve over localhost or HTTPS.
